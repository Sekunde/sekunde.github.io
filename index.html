Bio

I am a Principal Researcher at Tencent Hy, working on world models. Previously, a Staff Research Scientist at Meta Superintelligence Labs , working on foundation models. I did my Ph.D. at TUM Visual Computing Group headed by Prof. Matthias Niessner, where I work on Computer Vision and 3D Scene Understanding. During my Ph.D., I did an internship at Facebook AI Research (FAIR) with Prof. Saining Xie and Dr. Benjamin Graham on 3D representation learning.
